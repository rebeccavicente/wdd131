Sweet Legacy Cookies - Site Plan

Site Name

🍪 Sweet Legacy Cookies

I chose the name Sweet Legacy Cookies because my website is about a fictional family-owned bakery that has been making homemade cookies for over 30 years. The word Legacy represents family traditions and recipes that have been passed down through generations.

Optional Domain: sweetlegacycookies.com

Site Purpose

Sweet Legacy Cookies is a fictional bakery website where visitors can learn about the bakery's history, browse cookie flavors, discover the premium ingredients used in every recipe, find store and delivery information, and contact the bakery.

Scenarios

  • What cookie flavors does Sweet Legacy Cookies offer?
  • Does the bakery offer delivery or local pickup?

Color Schema

  • Warm Brown (#8B5E3C): Header, navigation, buttons, and headings.
  • Cream (#FFF8EE): Page background.

Typography

  • Playfair Display: Headings.
  • Poppins: Body text, navigation, and buttons.
